# Stage 12: Business & Clinical Impact Analysis
* **HHS OCR Precedent:** Comprehensive Neurology, P.C. paid **$25,000** in April 2025 and entered a multi-year Corrective Action Plan after ransomware compromised **~6,800 patients' ePHI**.
* **Clinic Downtime Impact:** A 5-day EHR/billing outage costs an outpatient neurology clinic `$65,000–$110,000` in deferred claims, manual charting delays, and breach notification overhead.
