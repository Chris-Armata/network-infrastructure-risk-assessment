# Stage 1: Scope, Authorization & Clinical Safety ROE

Modeled on the October 2023 ransomware attack against **TransForm Shared Service Organization (SSO)**—a shared IT company supporting five hospitals in southwestern Ontario (Bluewater Health, Chatham-Kent Health Alliance, Erie Shores HealthCare, Hôtel-Dieu Grace Healthcare, and Windsor Regional Hospital), investigated by **IPC Ontario** under **PHIPA** (Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act).
