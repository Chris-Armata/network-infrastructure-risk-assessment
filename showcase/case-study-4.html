Case Study 4: TransForm Shared Service Organization (SSO) — Multi-Hospital Supply-Chain Risk

Modeled on the October 2023 ransomware attack affecting five Ontario hospitals (IPC Ontario / PHIPA). Explains in plain English how one master key at an outside IT company knocks five hospitals offline—and how Zero Trust per-hospital isolation stops the spread.

BEFORE: One Master Key Across 5 Hospitals

MASTER_ADMIN_KEY="ACTIVE_FOR_ALL_5_HOSPITALS"
MFA_REQUIRED="FALSE"
{"hospital":"Bluewater","status":"503 OFFLINE - RANSOMWARE LOCKED"}
